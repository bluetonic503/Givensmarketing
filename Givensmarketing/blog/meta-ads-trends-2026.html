Meta Ads Trends in 2026

July 2026 7 min read Givens Marketing

Meta's advertising platform keeps shifting toward broader automation, and 2026 is no exception. Here is what has actually changed in how successful accounts are being run this year, rather than what has stayed the same in name only.

Broad targeting is the default, not the exception

Advantage+ style audience expansion continues to outperform narrow, manually built audiences for most advertisers. The algorithm has more signal to work with than manual segmentation typically allows, so the advertiser's job has shifted from building audiences to feeding the system enough creative variety and clean conversion data to work with.

Creative volume matters more than creative perfection

Accounts that consistently ship new creative variations tend to outperform accounts with a handful of highly polished but rarely refreshed ads. Quantity of relevant, on-brand variations has become a bigger lever than any single "perfect" ad.

Short-form video keeps gaining ground

Reels placements continue to take a larger share of impressions, and ads built natively for a vertical, sound-on, fast-paced format consistently outperform repurposed static or landscape content.

First-party data is doing more of the heavy lifting

With signal loss from privacy changes still a factor, accounts with clean conversions API setups and strong first-party data collection have a real targeting advantage over accounts relying on pixel data alone.

AI-assisted creative testing is standard practice

More advertisers are using AI tools to generate and test creative variations quickly, then relying on performance data rather than intuition to decide what scales. The advantage now sits with whoever tests the most disciplined, structured way, not necessarily whoever has the biggest budget.

The throughline: the platform is rewarding advertisers who feed it more signal, more creative variety, and cleaner data, and stepping back from advertisers who try to out-manual the algorithm.
